Fundamentals

A third overlay, same pattern as the other two

You've already built two things that pause the overworld and take over the screen: self.dialog_tree (a conversation) and self.battle (a fight). self.evolution is a third overlay, checked in run() exactly the same way: if self.evolution: self.evolution.update(dt).

The Evolution class itself (evolution.py) is completely pre-built — it manages its own two-stage animation using Timers, the same class you used back in session 2. Your job today is entirely about when to create one and what to do when it's done, not how the animation itself works.

Fundamentals — Step 3

check_evolution(): spotting the moment

Already wired up for you: end_battle() calls check_evolution() right after a wild encounter ends (not a trainer battle — that shows the trainer's defeat line instead). Your job is what happens inside that method.

Loop every monster on your team. The condition is exactly what you'd expect: does it have an evolve value, and does its current level match the level that evolution requires? monster.evolution == monster.evolution — wait, it's simpler: monster.level == monster.evolution[1].

Once you find one: build the Evolution overlay (it needs the monster's current name, its evolved name, and a callback for when it's done), then immediately replace that team slot with a fresh Monster of the evolved species — same level, new species. Then stop looking; one evolution at a time is plenty.

def check_evolution(self):
    for index, monster in self.player_monsters.items():
        if monster.evolution and monster.level == monster.evolution[1]:
            self.audio['evolution'].play()
            self.player.block()
            self.evolution = Evolution(
                self.monster_frames['monsters'],
                monster.name,
                monster.evolution[0],
                self.fonts['bold'],
                self.end_evolution,
                self.start_animation_frames)
            self.player_monsters[index] = Monster(monster.evolution[0], monster.level)
            break
    if not self.evolution:
        self.audio['overworld'].play(-1)
